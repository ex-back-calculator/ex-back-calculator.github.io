About ExBackChance

The psychology, the data, and the team behind the tool.

Welcome to ExBackChance. We built this platform because navigating a breakup is one of the most emotionally confusing experiences a person can go through. During this time, people often search for clarity, looking for answers to questions like, "Does my ex miss me?" or "What are the real chances we get back together?"

While no tool can guarantee a specific human outcome, we believe in providing a private, structured, and psychology-backed way to analyze your situation.

Our Methodology

Our calculator is not a random number generator. The algorithm is a proprietary weighted model built on established psychological frameworks regarding human attachment and relationship dynamics. Specifically, it analyzes your inputs across five core dimensions:

  • Attachment Theory: How secure or anxious-avoidant dynamics are playing a role in the separation.
  • The No-Contact Rule: Research on how periods of silence affect the "dumper's" psychological state over time.
  • Breakup Timeline: The statistical likelihood of reconciliation based on whether the breakup was recent or months ago.
  • Initiator Dynamics: Whether you ended it, they ended it, or it was mutual drastically alters the recovery curve.
  • Emotional State: Factoring in your current mood to provide tailored advice on your next steps.

Privacy-First Architecture

We understand that your relationship data is deeply personal. That is why we built our tool with a 100% client-side privacy architecture. When you enter names and answer questions, that data is processed entirely within your own browser. It is never uploaded to our servers, it is never stored in a database, and it is never shared with third parties.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to provide a private, engaging, and insightful tool that helps you process a breakup and, ultimately, move forward with clarity. Whether your result gives you the closure to move on or the strategy to try again, we are here to provide the data to help you make that decision.

A Note on Professional Help

While our tool is rooted in psychological concepts, it is designed for entertainment and informational purposes. It is not a substitute for professional therapy. If you are struggling deeply with a breakup or your mental health, we strongly encourage you to seek out a licensed counselor or therapist.
