The Psychology of Post-Breakup Communication

Reaching out to an ex is rarely just about the words you type. It is driven by a neurochemical urge for validation. When you text your ex, you are essentially asking them to soothe an anxiety that you should be soothing yourself. The "Should I Text My Ex" analyzer evaluates your timing, emotional state, and motivations to prevent you from making a text you will regret tomorrow.

The 24-Hour Rule

Before you send any text to an ex, you must apply the 24-hour rule. If you type out a message at 11 PM and still want to send it the next morning at 11 AM, it might be worth sending. If you wake up feeling relieved that you didn't send it, you successfully dodged a bullet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it ever a good idea to text an ex first? ▼

Only if the breakup was mutual, time has passed (at least 30 days), you are completely sober, and you have zero expectations about how they will reply.

Why do I feel a physical urge to text them? ▼

Your brain is going through dopamine withdrawal. You are literally addicted to the reward loop of interacting with them, and your brain is convincing you that a text will make the pain stop. It won't.
